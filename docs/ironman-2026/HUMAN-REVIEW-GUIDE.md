## Suggested review order

1. Read Days 01, 10, 20, and 30 to evaluate the four-act spine: origin, evidence transport, first weak layer, and current boundary.
2. Review the five `REVISE` articles: Days 01, 03, 06, 09, and 10.
3. Inspect all ten visuals from the article links or the internal contact sheet.
4. Review exact numbers in Days 15–30 against the publication manifest and frozen packets.
5. Approve titles and voice separately from factual readiness. Approval must be explicit; no repository status currently claims `APPROVED_BY_USER`.
